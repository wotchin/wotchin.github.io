About

Jack’s AI Studio is where I write about AI systems, AI agent, and infrastructure software from an engineering-first point of view.

My focus is the space where data systems meet modern AI: database kernels, scalable infrastructure, agent and LLM infra, representation learning, and the practical trade-offs behind production-grade systems.

I use this site for long-form technical notes, research digests, and implementation reflections.

I am most interested in:

  • AI infrastructure and data system architecture
  • Database performance, query processing, and emerging hardware
  • Technical research translation for engineering teams
  • System design review for early-stage products
